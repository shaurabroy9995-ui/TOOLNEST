About ToolNest

ToolNest provides fast, privacy-friendly browser-based utilities that help you accomplish everyday tasks instantly. From merging PDFs to generating QR codes, from resizing images to encoding text — no accounts, no friction, no data collection.

🔒

100% Private

All processing happens in your browser. We never store or see your data.

⚡

Lightning Fast

No server requests. Instant results. Zero latency.

🆓

Completely Free

No subscriptions, no hidden costs, no ads (unless you want to support us).
