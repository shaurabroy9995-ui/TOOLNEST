Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our priority. Learn how we protect your data.

Last Updated: January 13, 2025

Our Privacy Commitment

At ToolNest, we believe that privacy is a fundamental right. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information and what measures we take to protect your privacy. In short: We don't collect, store, or sell your personal data.

How ToolNest Works

All ToolNest tools process data entirely in your browser. This means:

  • Your files and data never leave your device
  • No data is sent to our servers
  • Your processing happens entirely offline (once the page is loaded)
  • We cannot see, access, or store what you process

What Data We Collect

Information We Don't Collect

  • ❌ Your files or documents (images, PDFs, text, etc.)
  • ❌ Personal information (names, emails, addresses)
  • ❌ Payment information (unless you donate)
  • ❌ Login credentials or account information
  • ❌ Browsing history or usage patterns of specific files
  • ❌ Location data or device identifiers

Limited Information We May Collect

We may collect non-personal, aggregated information for site improvement and analytics:

  • Page Views: Which pages you visit (not what you do on them)
  • Tool Usage: Which tools are used (not what files are processed)
  • Technical Information: Browser type, OS, screen resolution
  • Referral Source: How you found our site
  • Performance Metrics: Page load times, error rates

This information is collected through Google Analytics and similar services with privacy-focused settings enabled. We explicitly disable features that would track individual users.

How We Use Information

Any non-personal data we collect is used solely for:

  • ✓ Improving site performance and user experience
  • ✓ Identifying and fixing technical issues
  • ✓ Understanding which tools are most popular
  • ✓ Detecting and preventing abuse or attacks
  • ✓ Complying with legal obligations

We never sell, trade, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies & Tracking

Essential Cookies

We use only essential cookies necessary for the site to function:

  • toolnest-theme - Your preferred color scheme (light/dark)
  • toolnest-consent - Whether you've accepted our privacy policy

These cookies are stored only on your device and never sent to external servers.

Analytics Tracking

We use Google Analytics 4 with the following privacy protections:

  • IP anonymization enabled (last octet masked)
  • User identification disabled
  • Data retention limited to 14 months
  • Cross-site tracking disabled

You can opt out of Google Analytics by:

  • Installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on
  • Adjusting your browser's "Do Not Track" settings

Third-Party Services

We use these services with privacy considerations:

  • Google Analytics: Aggregated traffic analytics (anonymized)
  • Cloudflare: CDN and DDoS protection
  • GitHub: Source code hosting (public repository only)

None of these services have access to the data you process through ToolNest.

Data Security

We take security seriously:

  • 🔒 All connections use HTTPS encryption
  • 🔒 Service Worker enables offline functionality and caching
  • 🔒 No data is ever sent over unencrypted connections
  • 🔒 Regular security audits and code reviews
  • 🔒 Compliance with industry security standards

Your Rights

Under various privacy laws (GDPR, CCPA, etc.), you have rights including:

  • Right to Access: Request what data we have about you (minimal to none)
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of cookies and stored preferences
  • Right to Opt-Out: Disable analytics and tracking
  • Right to Portability: Export your data
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Change your preferences anytime

To exercise these rights, contact us.

Children's Privacy

ToolNest is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children. If we learn that a child has provided personal information, we will delete it promptly.

International Compliance

We comply with major privacy regulations:

  • GDPR (EU): General Data Protection Regulation
  • CCPA (California): California Consumer Privacy Act
  • LGPD (Brazil): Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados
  • PIPEDA (Canada): Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act

Open Source & Transparency

ToolNest is open source. You can:

  • 🔓 Review our source code on GitHub
  • 🔓 Verify what data is collected and how
  • 🔓 Audit our privacy practices
  • 🔓 Run ToolNest locally for complete privacy

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy occasionally. When we make material changes, we'll:

  • Update the "Last Updated" date
  • Notify users via email (if we have contact info)
  • Request renewed consent for significant changes

Your continued use of ToolNest indicates acceptance of any changes.
